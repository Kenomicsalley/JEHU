# JEHU Arena — Design Brief

## Working title
**JEHU Arena: Pressure Protocol**

## Player fantasy
“You and the people you care about are defenders. A scammer only needs one person to make the wrong decision. Can your household recognize the pressure before it wins?”

## Core loop
Receive → Inspect → Decide → Lock → Reveal → Debrief → Score → Rematch

## Modes
### Defender Duel
Every player competes individually.

### Household Shield
Future cooperative mode:
- Household has one shared “shield meter”.
- Each correct response repairs/strengthens the shield.
- Wrong decisions create simulated exposure.
- The household wins if its shield survives the final round.

## Scoring philosophy
Correctness dominates speed. This prevents the game from teaching “click fast” as a security habit.

Suggested future scoring:
- Correct: 700 base
- Speed bonus: up to 300
- Correct streak: +75 per streak
- Team rescue: +100
- Wrong: small participation XP, no punitive humiliation
