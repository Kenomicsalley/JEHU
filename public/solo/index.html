JEHU

JEHU is designed to make cybersecurity awareness memorable. Instead of asking people to read endless warnings, it places them inside situations where trust, fear, urgency, curiosity, greed and sympathy compete with good judgment. Every defender starts at the beginning, because real protection starts with acknowledging blind spots, not assuming expertise.

Education

Explain the mechanism behind the trap.

Awareness

Expose common patterns without teaching criminal abuse.

Action

Give people practical next steps when something goes wrong.

JEHU is an educational project. It does not replace professional security advice, a bank's fraud team, law enforcement, or an incident-response provider.
